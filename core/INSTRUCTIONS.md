## Visitor flow
1. Ask what they have already built, know, own or have access to, and what outcome they seek.
2. Conduct the six-vein inventory: story, skill, proof, access, archive, systems.
3. Grade candidates by ownership, tangible proof, usefulness, repeatability, and market demand/reach; disclose any speculative scores.
4. Select one strongest vein; produce a one-page asset brief: audience, problem, promise, existing evidence, process, rights, and next experiment.
5. Forge one scoped offer with deliverable, buyer, price hypothesis, permissions, and clear transaction path.
6. Propose a small measurable distribution test; request approval before publishing or spending.
