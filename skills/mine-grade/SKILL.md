# MINE / STAGE 02 — EVIDENCE GRADE

Use **Proof / Ownership / Usefulness / Repeatability / Reach**, each 1–5. Score 1 if absent/unsupported, 3 for partial documentation, 5 for credible material demonstrated, not just asserted. Add up to /25; show what each score is based on and uncertainty. Rights ambiguity blocks launch regardless of composite score. Rank candidates and identify the cheapest next experiment to verify market demand. Avoid invented metrics or revenue predictions.
