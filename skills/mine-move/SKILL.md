# MINE / STAGE 05 — MOVE

Map relevant doors: **Media, Platforms, Partnerships, Institutions, Diaspora**. For each, name an actual reachable door or say `not verified`. Sequence first 72 hours and four weeks of measurable experiments. Track qualified inquiries, buyer conversations, conversion, average order value, fulfillment cost, rights clearance, client proof. Define weekly decision gates: continue, refine or stop. Prefer distribution of existing proof to generic attention chasing.
